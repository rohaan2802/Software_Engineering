Overview

Software Engineering at FAST-NUCES Islamabad, Spring 2026. The repository has 14 lectures, four assignments with three Scrum sprint reports, the LibraryMS final project report, quizzes, class activities and exam papers, two textbooks and my study notes.

Unit 1

Process

Software engineering basics, lifecycle activities and the traditional process models.

Unit 2

Agile & requirements

Agile, XP and Scrum, requirements engineering, user stories and specifications.

Unit 3

Testing

Error, fault and failure, black-box and white-box testing.

Unit 4

Management & architecture

Project management, WBS, Gantt charts, the critical path method and architectural styles.
